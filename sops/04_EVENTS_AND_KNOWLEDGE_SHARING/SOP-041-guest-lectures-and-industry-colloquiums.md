## 1. Purpose
To connect Nalanda AI Club members directly with leading global researchers, practitioners, policymakers, and founders without bureaucratic delays or superficial ceremonial pomp.

---

## 2. Guiding Philosophy
* **Honest Practice Over Hype**: We invite people doing the real, grinding work—training models, wrestling with messy datasets, navigating regulatory ambiguity. Marketing talks and product sales pitches are barred.
* **Respect for Time**: All talks are tightly capped at 45 minutes of content + 25 minutes of honest, unscripted Q&A.

---

## 3. The 4-Week Event Execution Runbook

```
  T - 28 Days              T - 14 Days              T - 3 Days              T = 0 (Live)
  (Outreach & Invite)      (Abstract & Logistics)   (Dry-Run & Tech)        (Host & Record)
       │                        │                        │                       │
       ▼                        ▼                        ▼                       ▼
  Formal outreach          Flyer released;          Zoom/Campus audio       Session moderated;
  sent with Nalanda        Room booked or           verified; questions     Q&A facilitated;
  context                  link created             crowdsourced            recording stored
```

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Procedure

### Phase 1: Outreach (T - 28 Days)
* Use the standardized invite letter (`templates/guest-speaker-invite.md`).
* Highlight the unique interdisciplinary profile of the Nalanda cohort (MSc Data Science & AI).
* Inquire if speaker requires formal Nalanda University official invitation or honorarium.

### Phase 2: Logistics & Campus Protocol (T - 14 Days)
* If on-campus (Rajgir):
  1. Coordinate with Nalanda University guest house and travel desk.
  2. Reserve campus auditorium or seminar hall.
* If virtual:
  1. Create official meeting link with waiting room and recording enabled.
  2. Test screen-sharing, audio quality, and live captioning.

### Phase 3: The Live Session
* Moderator introduces speaker with focus on recent technical or policy contributions (not a 10-minute CV reading).
* Encourage students from diverse backgrounds to ask questions from their specific disciplines.

### Phase 4: Post-Event Gratitude & Archival (Within 48 Hours)
* Dispatch formal digital letter of gratitude signed by the Convenor and Faculty Advisor.
* Upload high-definition recording and presentation deck to the Club's private/public archive.
