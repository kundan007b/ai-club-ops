## 1. Purpose
To extend the benefits of modern data science and artificial intelligence across the entirety of Nalanda University (School of Historical Studies, School of Ecology and Environment Studies, School of Buddhist Studies, Philosophy & Comparative Religions, School of Management Studies).

---

## 2. The "AI for Humanities & Social Sciences" Clinic
* **Format**: Hands-on Saturday workshop (3 hours).
* **Modules**:
  1. *Prompt Engineering & Synthetic Research Assistants*: How to query unstructured historical archives without hallucination.
  2. *Data Wrangling without Code*: Modern spreadsheets, OpenRefine, and automated transcription tools (Whisper).
  3. *Network Analysis & Visualization*: Gephi and spatiotemporal mapping of trade routes and historical letters.

---

## 3. Workshop Delivery Checklist
* [ ] No local installation barriers: All participants run via browser-based cloud notebooks (Google Colab / JupyterLite).
* [ ] Real-world datasets provided: Example datasets from Bihar archaeological sites, Buddhist council texts, or rural micro-credit data.
* [ ] Minimum 1 TA per 5 attendees to provide immediate shoulder-to-shoulder assistance.
* [ ] Digital certificate of completion co-signed by Club Leads and School Dean.
