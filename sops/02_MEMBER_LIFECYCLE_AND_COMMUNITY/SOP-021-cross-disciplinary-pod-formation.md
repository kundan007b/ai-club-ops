## 1. Purpose
To prevent insular, purely speculative, or toy software engineering projects by establishing cross-disciplinary pairing as an institutional standard for all incubator tracks.

---

## 2. The Core Philosophy
> *"An algorithm in search of a problem is fragile; a deep real-world inquiry powered by modern computation changes understanding."*

Most engineering university clubs suffer from building redundant clone apps or Kaggle benchmarks with zero contextual meaning. Nalanda University's unique postgraduate demographic—spanning international relations, economics, peace studies, historical archives, ecology, and data science—is its single greatest competitive advantage.

---

## 3. The "2-in-a-Pod" Composition Rule
Every official Club research team, capstone squad, or hackathon delegation must meet the **Minimum Interdisciplinary Threshold**:

1. **At least ONE Computational Lead**:
   - Competency: Python, PyTorch/TensorFlow, SQL, API engineering, or statistical modelling.
   - Primary role: Architecture, data pipelines, model fine-tuning, reproducibility, and deployment.
2. **At least ONE Domain Specialist**:
   - Competency: International relations, economics, sociology, historical text analysis, ecology, agriculture, or public policy.
   - Primary role: Formulation of the core hypothesis, identification of domain-authentic datasets, error analysis, policy context, and qualitative validity.

---

## 4. Pod Formation Workflows

### Method A: Self-Assembled Pods
* Members browse the public directory (`directory.html`), filtering by complementary skills (e.g. filter by `Econometrics` + `Deep Learning`).
* The duo submits a 1-page Project Proposal via `templates/project-proposal-template.md`.

### Method B: The Monthly "Idea Collider"
* Held on the first Friday evening of each month on the Nalanda campus.
* Format:
  - 3 minutes: Domain specialists present 1 unresolved question or data challenge (e.g. "Mapping ancient trade routes from Pali/Sanskrit palm-leaf manuscripts" or "Predicting groundwater depletion in South Bihar").
  - 3 minutes: Technical students pitch algorithmic techniques (e.g. "Vision transformers for OCR on degraded scripts" or "Spatiotemporal graph neural networks").
  - 20 minutes: Open mixer to finalize pod pairings.

---

## 5. Pod Operational Cadence
* **Sprint Length**: 2-week agile iterations.
* **Weekly Sync**: Maximum 20 minutes (async Slack/WhatsApp updates preferred).
* **Parity of Authorship**: When papers, policy briefs, or software are published:
  - Domain and technical co-leads share primary co-first authorship.
  - No member is relegated to an acknowledgement footnote simply because they contributed domain formulation rather than git commits.
