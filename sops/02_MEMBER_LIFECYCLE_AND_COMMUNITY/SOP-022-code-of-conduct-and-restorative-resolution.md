## 1. Purpose
To ensure an inclusive, intellectually fearless, and psychologically safe environment where members of all backgrounds—regardless of coding experience, nationality, gender, or academic discipline—can collaborate without harassment, elitism, or fear of asking elementary questions.

---

## 2. Standards of Community Conduct

### Expected Behaviors
* **Assume Positive Intent & Curiosity**: Welcome different intellectual traditions without condescension.
* **No Question Gatekeeping**: Mockery of introductory coding or mathematical questions ("you don't even know that?") is strictly intolerable.
* **Active Listening Across Disciplines**: Respect the time and cognitive context of both domain experts and engineers.
* **Constructive Disagreement**: Critique hypotheses, algorithms, and models rigorously; never attack individual character or credentials.

### Unacceptable Behaviors
* Technical elitism or gatekeeping based on undergrad discipline or programming language.
* Plagiarism, fabricated data, or passing off unmodified generative AI outputs as original research.
* Harassment, sexist remarks, racial/caste prejudice, unwanted personal advances, or exclusion based on national origin.
* Unauthorized sharing of private member contact details or academic evaluations.

---

## 3. The 3-Tier Restorative Resolution Process
Rather than arbitrary bans or bureaucratic tribunals, the Club follows a **Restorative Justice Model**:

```
  Tier 1: Informal Peer Mediation
  (Within 48 hours: 1-on-1 private dialogue facilitated by a neutral steward)
        │
        ▼ If unresolved or repeated
  Tier 2: Restorative Circle
  (Conducted by Convenor + Domain Steward; structured plan to repair harm)
        │
        ▼ In cases of severe breach, harassment, or persistent misconduct
  Tier 3: Executive Escalation & University Referral
  (Revocation of Club privileges & referral to Nalanda University Proctorial Board)
```

### Protocol for Filing a Concern
1. Any member may email `aiclub@nalandalibrary.com` with the subject tag `[CONFIDENTIAL - CONDUCT]` or approach the Convenor or any Domain Steward directly.
2. The receiving steward must acknowledge receipt within 12 hours.
3. Anonymity of the reporting party is strictly protected throughout the preliminary review.
4. Both parties are offered a mediated conversation focused on understanding impact and agreeing on mutual behavioral commitments.
