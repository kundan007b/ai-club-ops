## 1. Purpose
To flatten steep learning curves in mathematical foundations, Python syntax, and empirical research methods through structured, judgment-free peer-to-peer assistance.

---

## 2. The Two-Way Mentorship Model
Mentorship in the AI Club is reciprocal:
* **The "Syntax-to-Semantics" Exchange**: A student from CS or physics mentors an economics/humanities student through Python, Git, and PyTorch tensors.
* In return, the economics/humanities student mentors the engineer through econometrics, research design, policy framing, and qualitative evaluation.

---

## 3. Operational Mechanics

### 3.1 Weekly "Zero-Judgement" Debugging Clinic
* **When**: Every Wednesday, 17:00 – 19:00 IST (Library common space / virtual hybrid).
* **Format**:
  - Open tables with power strips and monitors.
  - "Stuck Station": Members bring broken code, environment setup bugs (CUDA, conda, venv), or confusing statistical proofs.
  - Zero preparation needed; drop in anytime.

### 3.2 Mock Interview & Career Circles (Placement Season)
* In coordination with the Operations Steward, peer mock interviews are conducted bi-weekly during September–November and January–March.
* Tracks:
  1. **Applied Machine Learning & Data Engineering**: Live coding, SQL queries, system design.
  2. **Quantitative & Empirical Research**: Research design, econometric intuition, causal inference.
  3. **Policy & Strategy**: Case studies, ethical analysis, presentation skills.

### 3.3 Statement of Purpose (SOP) & Fellowship Reviews
* For members applying to international PhD programs, Rhodes/Fulbright/Chevening scholarships, or research lab internships, the Club organizes a blind peer review circle providing line-by-line editorial feedback.
