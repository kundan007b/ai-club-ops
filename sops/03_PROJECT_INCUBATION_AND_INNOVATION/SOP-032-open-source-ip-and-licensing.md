# SOP-032: Open Source, Intellectual Property, and Licensing Policy

| Metadata | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SOP Code** | SOP-032 |
| **Category** | 03 - Project Incubation & Innovation |

| **Target Audience** | All Creators, Authors, Project Leads, Legal Liaisons |
| **Principle** | "Students own their creations; community code belongs to humanity." |

---

## 1. Purpose
To eliminate IP ambiguity, encourage daring technical exploration, and safeguard student startup founders while maintaining robust open-source licensing for community-incubated work.

---

## 2. Core IP Tenets

### 2.1 100% Student Creator Ownership
* **Zero Club Claim**: The AI Club, Nalanda University, asserts zero ownership, zero equity, and zero royalty claims over software, datasets, models, patents, or business ventures initiated by its members.
* **Commercialization Freedom**: Student pods wishing to spin out their capstone or project into a commercial startup or patent are free to do so at their sole discretion.

### 2.2 Dual Open-Source Default for Community Work
For public projects utilizing shared Club resources, the following licensing matrix applies:

| Artifact Type | Mandatory License | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Source Code & Scripts** | **MIT** or **Apache 2.0** | Maximum permissiveness, patent grant (Apache), global adoption |
| **Datasets & Curricula** | **CC-BY 4.0** | Permissive reuse with mandatory attribution to authors |
| **Pretrained Weights** | **OpenRAIL-M** or **Apache 2.0** | Responsible AI usage restrictions without constraining research |
| **Internal Club SOPs** | **MIT / CC0** | Open knowledge sharing for any university to clone |

---

## 3. Nalanda University Institutional Attribution
All papers, preprints, and public repositories resulting from Club incubation must include the standardized attribution string:

> *"This work was conducted at the AI Club, MSc Data Science & AI Programme, Nalanda University, Rajgir, Bihar, India."*

---

## 4. Third-Party Code, Data, and License Compliance
1. **GPL / Copyleft Hygiene**: Members must exercise extreme care when incorporating GPL/AGPL libraries into commercializable code.
2. **Dataset Provenance**: Never scrape or ingest datasets with explicit restrictive terms of service without documenting an exemption or fair-use justification.
3. **Attribution File**: Every public repository must maintain an up-to-date `NOTICE` or `CREDITS.md` acknowledging open-source dependencies and contributors.
