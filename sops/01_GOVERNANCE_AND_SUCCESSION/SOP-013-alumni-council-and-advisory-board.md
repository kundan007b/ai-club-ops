---

## 1. Purpose
To retain the intellectual capital, professional networks, and institutional affinity of graduated cohorts (beginning with Cohort 2026–28) as lifelong assets for active Nalanda AI Club members.

---

## 2. Structure of the Alumni Council
1. **Automatic Inscription**: Every member who completes their MSc Data Science & AI degree and actively participated in at least one club pod or reading group is inducted into the **Nalanda AI Alumni Circle**.
2. **Advisory Stewards**: Two alumni representatives are elected annually by the alumni body to serve as consultative advisors to the active Convenor.
3. **Faculty Anchor**: The Faculty Advisor serves as the permanent bridge connecting past cohorts, active students, and university administration.

---

## 3. Operational Engagements

### 3.1 The "Alumni Office Hours" (Monthly)
* On the third Saturday of each month, alumni working in research labs, policy institutes, civil services, or AI industry host 45-minute virtual sessions.
* Topics include: honest interview breakdowns, production system design, grant writing, and academic PhD application debriefs.

### 3.2 Placement & Research Referrals
* Alumni maintain a closed, vetted referral channel for club members.
* Pre-requisite for alumni referral: The candidate must have contributed code or domain research to at least one public Club repository or paper reading group session.

### 3.3 The Alumni Seed & Compute Grant
* Graduated members may voluntarily sponsor compute micro-grants (e.g. ₹5,000 / $60 GPU run budgets for student research papers).
* 100% of donated compute funds are tracked openly in `SOP-052: Public Ledger` and audited quarterly.

---

## 4. Archival and Legacy Contributions
* Alumni retain read access to all public repositories and community channels.
* Alumni are invited to return as evaluators for the annual Nalanda Interdisciplinary AI Showcase.
