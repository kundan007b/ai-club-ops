---

## 1. Purpose
To ensure that financial barriers never prevent a Nalanda student from running an experiment, hosting a guest, or attending a competition, while maintaining transparent double-entry accounting.

---

## 2. Eligible Expense Categories
1. **Cloud & Compute Costs**: RunPod, Lambda Labs, Google Cloud, OpenAI/Anthropic API credits.
2. **Competition Registration Fees**: Hackathons, Kaggle competition entry fees.
3. **Hardware Components**: Microcontrollers, sensors (e.g. for agricultural/environmental IoT AI projects).
4. **Guest Hospitality & Refreshments**: Tea/coffee for weekly reading groups or visiting speaker dinners.

---

## 3. Fast Reimbursement Protocol (Under ₹5,000)

```
  Step 1: Receipt Upload ──► Step 2: UPI Verification ──► Step 3: Ledger Entry
  (Member submits GST/       (Treasurer audits invoice    (Transaction recorded
   digital bill + UPI ID)     within 24h & initiates UPI)   in public ledger)
```

### Procedure:
1. Member emails `aiclub@nalandalibrary.com` with subject `[REIMBURSEMENT] - <Project/Event Name>`.
2. Attach: Tax invoice / receipt, brief note on purpose, and UPI ID.
3. Treasurer verifies the invoice and disburses payment via UPI within **48 hours**.
4. Disbursed amount is logged into `LEDGER.md` (SOP-052).

---

## 4. Expenses Exceeding ₹5,000
* Requires prior approval via a fast 24-hour steward quorum or standard RFC (SOP-012).
* Cash advances may be issued for major equipment purchases or travel stipends.
