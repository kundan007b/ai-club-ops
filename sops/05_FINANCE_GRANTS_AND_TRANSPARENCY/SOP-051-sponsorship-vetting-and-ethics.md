---

## 1. Purpose
To maintain academic integrity, student privacy, and institutional prestige by rigorously vetting all corporate sponsorships, cloud grant offers, and external financial partnerships.

---

## 2. Ineligible Sponsors (The Red List)
The Club strictly refuses sponsorship, co-branding, or prize donations from:
1. **Predatory EdTech / Essay Mills**: Platforms profiting from academic fraud or aggressive student debt.
2. **Deceptive Cryptocurrency / Web3 Schemes**: Unregulated token sales, speculative NFT schemes, or pump-and-dump projects.
3. **Surveillance & Weaponry Contractors**: Entities building autonomous weapons systems or non-consensual biometric tracking.
4. **Surreptitious Data Harvesters**: Companies demanding exclusive access to student CVs or personal research datasets as a condition of sponsorship.

---

## 3. Approved Partnership Formats
* **Unrestricted Grants**: Companies providing cloud computing credits (e.g. Google Cloud for Startups, AWS Educate, GitHub Education, Hugging Face) without demanding proprietary IP rights.
* **Hackathon Prizes**: Transparent prize money awarded openly based on objective technical judging.
* **Hardware Donations**: Donated edge devices (NVIDIA Jetson, Coral TPUs, Raspberry Pis) permanently added to the Club's hardware library.

---

## 4. Contract Review & Faculty Approval
* Any sponsorship agreement involving funds > ₹25,000 or formal legal memorandums of understanding (MOUs) must be approved by the Faculty Advisor and conform with Nalanda University institutional guidelines.
