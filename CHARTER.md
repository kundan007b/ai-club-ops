# 🏛️ The Founding Charter of the AI Club — Nalanda University

> *"Knowledge is the true wealth that grows when shared; true inquiry has no boundary between disciplines."*  
> — Inspired by the Nalanda Tradition (राजगृह / नालन्दा)

---

## Article I: Name and Identity
1. The collective shall be known as the **AI Club, Nalanda University** (hereinafter referred to as the "Club").
2. The Club is established under the aegis of the **MSc Data Science & AI Programme** at Nalanda University (Rajgir, Bihar, India), serving the founding Cohort 2026–28 and all subsequent graduating cohorts.
3. The official digital identity, documentation, and registry are anchored at `https://aiclub.nalandalibrary.com` and GitHub organisation `nalanda-ai-club`.
4. **Domain Ownership & Non-Transferability**: The root domain `nalandalibrary.com` (and all associated top-level infrastructure) is the exclusive, non-transferable private property of the Founder. The Club operates its digital presence on the delegated subdomain (`aiclub.nalandalibrary.com`). Under no circumstances is root domain ownership or registrar transfer permitted as part of cohort succession or governance transitions.

---

## Article II: Foundational Premise — The Multidisciplinary Imperative
1. **The Mixed-Background Reality**: Unlike conventional engineering-only collectives, the AI Club at Nalanda University brings together scholars from international relations, economics, history, humanities, ecological studies, commerce, computer science, and the physical sciences.
2. **Parity of Esteem**: Technical implementation and domain-specific inquiry are held in absolute equal regard. A sophisticated neural architecture addressing a trivial or ungrounded question is considered an incomplete inquiry; a piercing domain question without computational rigor is an incomplete inquiry. The strength of the Club lies in bridging the two.
3. **The "2-in-a-Pod" Matching Law**: All club incubation projects, case competition squads, and policy briefs must actively pair computational practitioners with domain specialists.

---

## Article III: Core Principles of Governance
1. **Default-to-Approve**: In order to maximize student velocity and drive relentless innovation, initiatives, experiments, and open-source explorations operate under a presumption of approval. Bureaucratic gatekeeping, multi-signature veto chokepoints, and artificial friction are explicitly prohibited.
2. **72-Hour Asynchronous Consent (RFC)**: Routine operational decisions, guidelines, and project approvals operate under the 72-hour silent consent model.
3. **Transparent & Open Ledger**: All finances, computing quotas, sponsorships, and organizational decisions are published openly to all members. Zero private club accounts or hidden deliberations.
4. **Perpetual Succession & The Relay Principle**: Student leadership is transient; institutional memory must be permanent. Outgoing cohorts owe incoming cohorts a mandatory 60-day structured handover, credential transfer, and advisory stewardship.
5. **Team Structure Stability & The 80% Consent Rule**: The organizational topology, executive roles, and task ownership matrix are defined in `TEAM_STRUCTURE.md` and `SOP-014`. Any proposal to alter the governance hierarchy, remove or add executive stewards, or reorganize core pod mandates requires an **affirmative supermajority consent of eighty percent (80%)** of the active cohort.

---

## Article IV: Intellectual Property & Public Good
1. **Creator Ownership**: All algorithms, models, publications, and startup intellectual property created by members remain 100% the property of their student creators. The Club claims zero equity or IP assignment.
2. **Open-by-Default Contribution**: Codebases developed under shared Club compute or community sprints shall be open-sourced under the **MIT** or **Apache 2.0** license. Curricula, reading notes, and research whitepapers shall be licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)**.
3. **Attribution & Academic Integrity**: All publications and projects incubated within the Club shall acknowledge *AI Club, Nalanda University* and strictly uphold international norms of plagiarism prevention, data authenticity, and citation rigor.

---

## Article V: Responsible AI & Civilizational Ethics
1. **Ethics Grounded in Purpose**: Members shall not design, train, or deploy systems intended for deceptive manipulation, weaponization, unauthorized surveillance, or academic fraud.
2. **Privacy & Sovereign Compliance**: All data collection, processing, and curation must comply with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** and ethical human-subject research standards.
3. **Critical Assessment**: Every deployed model or published study must include a standardized **Model Card** documenting training data provenance, evaluation metrics, limitations, and potential systemic biases.

---

## Article VI: Amendments
1. **Operational Amendments**: Standard procedural updates to individual SOPs may be enacted via a 72-hour RFC (SOP-012).
2. **Structural & Constitutional Amendments**: Amendments to this Charter, core governance rights, or the foundational Team Structure (`TEAM_STRUCTURE.md`) strictly require:
   - A mandatory 7-day campus comment period.
   - An affirmative supermajority vote of at least **80% of active cohort members**.
   - Review and endorsement by the Faculty Advisory Board.

*Ratified by the Founding Cohort (2026–28) at Nalanda University, Rajgir.*
